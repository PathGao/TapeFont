# Group 1 components (from hand-built glyphs)

Coordinates: x as fraction of cell width (0..1), y as fraction of cell height (0..1, y down). Tape = w (fraction of height).
Chars in brackets are where it was seen.

## General
- Two ways to make enclosures. Light tape: outline of strips (口 日). Heavy or dense: one solid rounded block with slits cut out (里's 田, 面, 署's 罒 and 日, 賢's 臣 and 貝, 場's 昜, 展's 共, 煙's 西). Slits are 0.3-0.5 tape tall (or 0.1 tape hairlines in 賢 貝), and start about 1 tape in from the block edge.
- Free ends square. Outer bends rounded about 1 tape. T-joins square. Wedge tips, arrow corners and flush-cut diagonal ends stay sharp. Some box corners are square in the photo (宀 top-left in 室, ワ top-left, 円 top-left).
- Diagonals are rare. Most ノ / 払い become a post with a foot or a flat bar (手 top, チ top, か, 佐 左).
- Leg wedges (人 / 木 / 火 legs) are long thin triangles hanging off the stem, cut flush to a vertical or horizontal edge (札 木, 術 朮, 煙 火, 場 土).

## Kana strokes
- Hook / bowl (か つ ら ワ サ ュ): bar, rounded bend, post, rounded bend, base bar. か's bowl: bar y 0.07 → post x 0.66 → short foot left at y 0.92.
- か ノ: post x 0.31 with foot to the left edge at y 0.92. か tick: short post x 0.87, y 0.02-0.44.
- ら: tab bar at top right (x 0.26-0.98, y 0.06), then post x 0.12 down to y 0.38, bend, bar right, bend, post x 0.87 down, bend, base bar back to x 0.03 at y 0.93.
- つ: bar y 0.14, post x 0.79, base bar y 0.84 (reversed C), 2 rounded bends.
- ぷ (ふ): three posts (left x 0.13, right x 0.81, from y 0.31 to the bottom). Middle stroke is a zigzag: top hook bar, post, a kink left, then a loop into a base bar. Handakuten = disc (r ~0.1 of height) with a pin hole.
- エ: two bars + stem, square T-joins.
- チ: heavy tape: two bars separated only by a hairline slit; stem hooks left into a foot with a large-radius outer curve.
- ヌ, ヘ: heavy tape, diagonals as tapered blocks with ends cut flush to the cell edges.
- ワ: no diagonal: left stub, bar, right post, base bar (box-like hook).
- サ: full bar y 0.23, left short post, right post hooking left into a base bar at y 0.91.
- ュ (small): bar + post bend in the lower half, base bar spans the full cell and continues into the next one.

## Latin, marks
- H: two posts + crossbar, square joins (b138 heavy, w 0.235).
- T: small cap sits low; bar y 0.27, stem down to the baseline.
- 7: bar, then one diagonal strip down-left into a vertical stem, rounded bends.
- !: half-disc top tapering to a point at y 0.70, disc dot at y 0.89 (r 0.08).
- ↖: one cut block: triangle head plus a wide 45-degree shaft cut flush to the right and bottom edges. All its corners are sharp (no rounding).
