# Group 3 components

Coordinates are strip centerlines in the unit cell: x = fraction of cell width, y = fraction of cell height (y down).
w = tape width / cell height. Source glyph JSONs are in glyphs/manual/ (multiply x there by 1/aspect to get these numbers).

## Counters: solid block + hairline slit (the most common rule here)
Closed boxes are rarely drawn as thin rings. Sato packs parallel strips so close that the counter shrinks to a slit of 0.1-0.3 tape.
- 日/目 inside a larger glyph: N horizontal strips stacked with center spacing about 1.15-1.2 w, closed left and right by two stems. 宿 (百's 日): strips y .575/.76/.93, x .27-.98, stems x .33/.90. 現 (目): 4 strips y .12/.28/.44/.59, stems x .495/.90. 銀 (艮 top): 3 strips y .14/.33/.52. 真: one solid block x .175-.835, y .18-.80 with three cut slits (y .36/.47/.58, height .03, x .21-.80).
- 口 as a component: a box strip whose hole is a narrow slot. 品: top 口 is a horizontal slot (box x .275-.695, y .11-.31), bottom 口s are vertical slots (boxes x .10-.375 and .59-.885, y .475-.91). 崎 (可's 口): box x .51-.705, y .655-.80, slot nearly closed. 館: 𠂤 = two stacked boxes with slots on one stem.
- Loops in kana (る, ま) are nested boxes, not curves: る = tall L-box (x .16-.855, y .41 down) with a small inner box at the bottom right (x .61-.855, y .68-.93).
- 罒 (夢) is one rounded band with a full-width slit: box x .145-.875, y .255-.375.

## 亻
Stem plus a short left tab, no diagonal. 宿: stem x .13 from y .31 to 1.0, tab = block x 0-.07, y .48-.65. 袋 (亻 and the left half of 衣 are the same unit, stacked): stem x .275, tab bar from x .02 to the stem at y .295 (upper) and .755 (lower), outer corner rounded. 係: stem x .26, tab bar x .04-.26 at y .51.

## 氵
池 (b007): three equal short bars, x .02-.21, at y .145/.555/.925. There is no wedge on this sign, unlike the bars + bottom wedge in notes_batch3.

## 扌
換: top bar y .055 x .14-.40, middle bar y .505 x .03-.32, stem is one slanted strip from (.25,.03) to (.105,.91). This sign leans like italic, so the slant is not a general rule.

## 木 (left side, 松)
Bar y .175 x .03-.45. Stem x .25 full height. Left sweep: diagonal from the stem at (.17,.22) to the cell edge at (.035,.60), then straight down to the bottom. Right sweep: one diagonal (.31,.22)-(.45,.95).

## 糸 (紅)
L at top left (x .08, y .11-.30, foot to x .25). Second stroke: vertical x .24 y .10-.36, diagonal to (.07,.47), bar y .515 to x .40. Short vertical x .395 y .27-.64. Stem x .275 y .515-.86. Dots are two short vertical bars x .095 and .42, y .66-.86. 係's 糸 (low-res, unconfirmed) is bar + two zigzag diagonals + bar + stem + two splayed diagonals.

## 金 (銀)
Roof = two separate rounded blocks at y .14 (x .03-.23 and .27-.47). Bars y .34 and .53 across x .03-.47. Stem x .245 y .53-.85. Dots = two short opposing diagonals (.06,.64)-(.17,.80) and (.45,.64)-(.33,.78). Base bar y .90.

## 食 (館, b015 round style)
Roof = ∩ box x .075-.41 with legs to y .29. The 人 peak is a disc (r .07) sitting in the roof gap. Body = three bars y .36/.53/.70 between stems x .075/.41. Left stem continues to y .93 with a short foot. The tail is a disc (r .085) at (.37,.89) on a short diagonal.

## 宀 / 冖 / 广
- 宀 (宿): dot = short vertical tab x .485 y 0-.14; bar y .14 full width; two short legs, left x .11 to y .25, right x .885 to y .30, outer corners rounded.
- 冖 (夢): bar y .46, right leg down to y .76, left leg down to y .755 then a foot out to x 0.
- 广 (座): bar y .11 x .085-.98 with left stem x .085 down to y .93 (one strip, rounded top-left). Dot = tab x .58 above the bar.

## 艹 (夢)
Bar y .135 x .06-.92 with two short vertical tabs (x .24 and .68) crossing it, tops at y .03 and 0.

## 丷 / 八 on top (前)
Two small rectangular tabs sitting on the top bar (x .31-.42 and .58-.68, y .01-.06).

## 人 inside 座 (tent)
Vertical tab (x .35, y .21-.31) splitting into two short diagonals to (.22,.42) and (.50,.42). Same for the right 人 at x .80 with legs to .64 and .95.

## 大 / 夕 / 弋
- 大 in 奇 (崎): bar y .135, then two short splayed diagonals (.60,.21)-(.44,.34) and (.77,.21)-(.93,.34); the space between is a triangle-shaped counter.
- 大 in 換: bar y .505, left leg diagonal ending in a flat foot (.40,.875)-(.29,.875), right leg one diagonal to the bottom right.
- 夕 (夢): an n-shape: left stem x .31, bar y .565, right stem x .795 dropping to a bottom bar y .95 that runs back left to x .20.
- 弋 (袋): bar y .145; the hook is a U of strips (x .68 and .89, bottom y .34) with a hairline slot; dot = small rect at the top right.

## 月 (前) and 刂
月: left stem x .07 full height; right stem x .365 ending in an L-foot to x .23 at y .94; bars y .22/.475/.71. 刂: short vertical x .63 (y .22-.63) and a long one x .915 with an L-foot to x .60 at y .94.

## 山 (崎)
Three verticals x .055/.21/.40. The left one turns into the bottom bar at y .93. The right one runs to the cell bottom. The middle one is shorter (to y .83).

## 也 (池)
Bar y .145 from x .27 to a right column x .875 that turns back left at y .59 to the middle stem x .625. Left stem x .385 full height turns into the bottom bar y .925 and ends in an up-stub at x .875 (to y .75).

## 王 / 土 / 工 / 十
Plain bars on one stem. 現's 王 sits in the top 60% (bars y .12/.36/.58). 座's 土: stem x .605, bars y .54 and .86. 走's 土 shares its stem with 止's short right bar (y .625, x .57-.92).

## 儿 (現)
Two stems with L-feet pointing opposite ways: (.56, .59-.92) foot left to x .29; (.725, .59-.89) foot right to x .97.

## 习 (羽)
Γ: bar y .145 from x .03 to a column x .425 running down to the cell bottom, rounded corner. The ticks are two free short bars y .435 and .695, x .03-.31. The right 习 is the same at x .51/.90.

## 日 + 刀 + 口 (昭)
日 is a tall stadium (box x .19-.40, y .085-.935 plus middle bar y .455) with two tall slot counters. 刀 is a rounded ∩ (x .535-.805, y .06-.53). 口 is a rounded box with a square counter.

## Kana strokes
- Curves are polylines of H/V strips. Every outer bend is rounded about 1 tape. Inner bends are square.
- Heavy b017/b107/b108 kana (w .17-.26): ツ and ノ end in an L whose outer corner is a 45-degree chamfer (cut the corner along a line from about (.92,.70) to (.64,1.0)). The inner corner gets a small triangular fill. The bottom bar of the L is two strips thick.
- オ: bar y .185, stem x .60 that pokes above the bar to y .05 and ends in an L-foot to x .26. The diagonal is one strip cut flush vertical at x .07.
- シ/ジ: three stacked bars. The top two are short (x .05-.52). The bottom one is an L into a right stem (x .705 from y .42). Dakuten = two short square bars level with the top bar (x .58-.71 and .77-.88).
- ン (a012): short top bar plus a full-height L. No diagonal.
- ヨ: three bars on a right column with slits about 0.4 tape.
- マ (b017): top bar 2 strips thick, right column, lower-left block 2 strips. The large outer curve is cut away as a polygon.
- で: the top bar folds back on itself (slit between the two layers) and then makes an L down and out. Dakuten = two small discs (r .038 of H) stacked vertically.
- き: bars y .11/.55, one straight diagonal (.47,.03)-(.745,.66) that drops straight down to y .87. The bottom curve is an L (x .175, y .69-.895, then out to x .52).
- ま (narrow a034): the loop and the tail merge into one solid lobe right of the stem. Only a slot stays left of the stem.
- 。 = rounded rectangle ring with a slot counter. . = disc about 1.7 tape across on the baseline.

## Latin / digits
- 9 (heavy, w .2): bars y .135/.465/.86 on a right stem. The left stem is split by a gap (y .47-.61) above a bottom stub.
- J (a012): flag at the top, stem x .78, rounded foot, short left stub up to y .52.
- W (a036 small caps): 4 straight strips (two outer near-verticals plus two centre diagonals that overlap into a peak). The tops are cut flush at y .22.
