# Group 2 components

Coordinates: x in [0, aspect] given as a fraction u of cell width (x = u * aspect), y in [0, 1] down. w = tape width / cell height. "block" = solid filled rectangle (poly), "slit" = thin cut (about 0.01-0.02 H).

## Counters shrink to slits on heavy signs (口 日 目 田 呂)
- Heavy tape (w >= 0.13) or a crowded part: an enclosure is a solid rounded block and its counter is only a hairline slit or slot. Seen in 宮 (b102), 東 (a024), 県 (b015), 頭 (a040), 野 (b007).
- 口 in 呂 (宮): block u 0.21-0.80, y 0.33-0.65 with one centered slit y 0.48-0.495, u 0.40-0.63. Lower 口 is full width (u 0.02-0.98, y 0.67-0.99), slit u 0.17-0.82 at y 0.83. The two 口 are joined by a short stem (w wide) at the center.
- 日 in 東: stadium block u 0.01-0.93, y 0.28-0.59 (end radius about 2 tape, chamfer 0.08 u / 0.06 y then round). Two slit rows y 0.37-0.39 and 0.48-0.50, each split by the stem: u 0.16-0.39 and 0.55-0.75.
- 目 (県, 頁 in 頭): rounded block, three slits evenly spaced (県: y 0.20, 0.34, 0.49 inside a block y 0.06-0.62; 頭: y 0.41, 0.55, 0.69 inside y 0.28-0.80). Slits stop about 1 tape short of each side.
- 田 in 里 (野): one block, the four counters become four pinholes (about 0.03 x 0.04).
- Light tape (w <= 0.10) keeps a real box: 中 (a024) = closed rounded strip loop u 0.105-0.845, y 0.16-0.86, stem full height through it.
- 口 as a tall right part (和 b016): rounded loop strip, counter is a narrow slot.

## 日 / 月 side by side (明 b016)
- 日: loop strip u 0.195-0.405, y 0.065-0.655 + midbar 0.355. 月: loop u 0.53-0.795 with bars at 0.065, 0.355, 0.655, both legs run to 0.935 and hook left (left leg to u 0.34, right leg to u 0.66). 日 and 月 touch.

## 木 / 本 / 禾 / 米 legs
- 木 in 東: legs are straight diagonal polys from the stem foot of the 日 block to the cell edges, cut vertical at the outer end. Left: (0.39,0.585)-(0.39,0.72)-(0.0,1.0)-(0.0,0.86). Right mirrored to u 0.92. Outer ends sharp.
- 禾 in 和: the ノ becomes a full top bar (same length as the crossbar). Legs are long thin triangles: vertical inner edge on the stem side, vertical foot at the cell side: left (0.17,0.33)-(0.17,0.66)-(0.02,0.97)-(0.02,0.64).
- ホ (a038) uses the same triangle legs: (0.30,0.175)-(0.30,0.55)-(0.035,0.975)-(0.03,0.57).
- 本 in 体 (a042): A-frame. Two straight legs from just under the top bar at the stem to the bottom corners, plus a low crossbar at y 0.82 that closes the A. Stem full height.
- 米 in 粧 (a016): stem + bar at y 0.5 + four wedge arms. Arms are narrow (about 0.08 u) at their outer tips and widen to meet the stem, so the middle band y 0.3-0.7 reads almost solid. Tips sharp, cut horizontal.

## 大 / 小 / 赤 dots (blade pieces)
- 大 in 美: legs are long shallow diagonals from under the last bar at the center to the cell sides, cut vertical at u 0.01 and 0.97.
- 小 in 県: stem + two parallelograms cut horizontal at top (y 0.79) and bottom (y 1.0): left (0.19,0.79)-(0.37,0.79)-(0.195,1.0)-(0.015,1.0).
- 赤 bottom dots: 45-degree pieces with both ends cut vertical, blade tips sharp: left (0.21,0.56)-(0.21,0.80)-(0.02,0.99)-(0.02,0.75).

## 氵 (江 b016)
- Two short bars u 0.13-0.38 at y 0.15 and 0.44 (length about 2 tape), then a rising diagonal piece cut vertical at both ends: (0.14,0.848)-(0.39,0.648)-(0.39,0.808)-(0.14,0.99).

## 亻 (体 a042, 付 in 府 b139)
- One vertical strip, full height or from y 0.35, plus a short left tab (length about 1-1.5 tape) at mid height. No left-falling stroke.

## 彳 / 亍 (街 b125)
- 彳: short left stem u 0.065 y 0.06-0.47, bar at y 0.59 u 0.0-0.27, tall right stem u 0.22 y 0.05-0.96. No diagonals.
- 亍: two bars (y 0.12, 0.40), stem from the second bar down to y 0.885 with an L-hook left to u 0.65 (rounded outer bend).

## 土 / 圭 / 王
- 土: stem + two bars, top bar shorter or equal. 圭 (街, 掛) = two 土 stacked; the stems do not join (gap at the middle), bars equal length.
- 王 (a040): three full-width bars y 0.14, 0.505, 0.915 + stem. Middle bar is as long as the others.
- 赤 top: stub above the first bar, then a second full bar; lower part two stems.

## 广 (府 b139, 粧 a016)
- Stub at u 0.6-0.7 from the top. One strip: bar from the right edge to the left stem, rounded bend, stem down to y 0.93; in 府 it ends in a rounded foot to the left cell edge.

## 宀 / 冖
- 宀 (宮): stub at center + 冖 = bar with two legs dropping to about y 0.5, both outer corners rounded.
- 冖 (写 b129): bar y 0.18, legs to y 0.43, rounded corners.

## 扌 (掛, x_ sign, low confidence)
- Stem full height + one top bar + a long diagonal crossing the stem bottom-left to upper-right. No middle bar visible.

## 頁 (頭 a040)
- Top bar + center stub, 目 block (see above), legs are two short near-vertical pieces under the block, outer ends sharp.

## 夕 (外 x_a052)
- One strip: left stem up, rounded corner, top bar, right stem down to a foot that turns left. The dot is a doubled middle bar that leaves a small slot under the top bar.

## 予 (野 b007)
- Two lobes, each a bar bending down with a rounded right corner; the lower lobe closes back to the stem. Stem drops and hooks left at the foot.

## Kana strokes
- Hook / L (カ in が, り, ユ, シ, ヲ, ッ): one strip with a rounded outer bend; inner corner square. Feet of カ both turn left.
- Fold (て b137): one strip zig-zags: top bar, short drop at the right, back left as a second bar (a slit separates the bars), then down and right as the base.
- Ticks (ッ a034): parallelograms cut vertical at both ends, about 0.24 u wide, sloping down to the right.
- ネ / ヲ / シ on the b017 kana chart (wide cells, aspect 1.0-1.5, w 0.25-0.29): strokes become slabs separated by slits. シ's ticks are horizontal bars, ヲ is drawn like ヨ.
- へ: two legs as one poly with a rounded apex; short left leg cut vertical, long right leg cut flush to the baseline.
- ォ (small, b107): cross of bar + stem, left stroke a short wedge cut vertical.
- 、 (a034): one diagonal piece with both ends cut vertical, sloping down to the right.
- Dakuten (が b108): two short vertical bars at the top right (the right one squat), not discs here.
- 丷 in 美 (b015): two discs (diameter about 1.6 tape) sitting on the top bar.

## Latin / symbols
- 8 (b117): two stacked rounded boxes sharing the middle bar, the lower box slightly wider/offset left.
- U (b138): one U strip, rounded bottom corners. I (b120): single bar of w 0.2.
- # (a034): two slanted parallelograms (ends cut horizontal) + two horizontal bars, upper bar shifted right, lower bar shifted left.
